Method notes: what the signal-processing blocks mean
RMS

Energy estimate: square, average, then square-root.

STFT

Windowed Fourier transform used to analyse time-varying spectra.

Mel / MFCC

Mel bands approximate perceptual frequency spacing; MFCCs compactly describe the log-mel shape.

Mid / Side

Mid=(L+R)/2, Side=(L-R)/2. Side energy measures stereo difference.

Interpretation quick guide

Energy and activity

RMS dB and SNR-like show loudness and clarity. VAD = activity on or off.

Pitch and voicing

F0 mean tracks pitch; voiced ratio tracks how tonal the signal is.

Boundaries

Boundary confidence spikes indicate likely phrase breaks or transitions.
